## THE STRUCTURE

**Floor covenant: $4,495.50/month.** No Tier 2 partner may price a CID below the
Tier 1 referral price. The $4,995 list stays true on every route.

| Active CIDs | Wholesale | Partner pays | Margin at floor | /yr | Margin at list | /yr | AXIOM /yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–4 | 15% | 4,245.75 | 249.75 | 2,997 | 749.25 | 8,991 | 50,949 |
| 5–14 | 20% | 3,996.00 | 499.50 | 5,994 | 999.00 | 11,988 | 47,952 |
| 15+ | 25% | 3,746.25 | 749.25 | 8,991 | 1,248.75 | 14,985 | 44,955 |

**Prescience carries the same tier percentage** — 15/20/25% off $11,995,
giving partner margin of $21,591 / $28,788 / $35,985 per CID per year at list.

Reasoning: zero arbitrage is the standing principle. Carving Prescience out
would give a partner a reason to keep clients on Business, which is the opposite
of what the upgrade path is for. If the dedicated CSM makes Prescience
unprofitable at 25% off, **the answer is to reprice Prescience, not to except
it** — an exception makes the price mean two things depending on route, which is
the problem the covenant exists to prevent.

---

## THE ENTRY TIER MOVED FROM 10% TO 15%

The memo proposed 10% at the entry band. **Modelled against the covenant it
does not work:** a partner buying at 10% pays $4,495.50, which *is* the floor.
Their margin at the floor is exactly zero. They could only earn by selling above
the Tier 1 referral price, which makes Tier 2 the expensive route for a client
and inverts the whole proposition.

15% is the shallowest entry band that leaves a positive margin at the covenant.

---

## THE INCENTIVE THIS CREATES — INTENTIONAL

Months for a Tier 2 partner to beat Tier 1's $5,394.60 one-off:

| Band | Selling at the floor | Selling at list |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–4 | 21.6 months | 7.2 months |
| 5–14 | 10.8 months | 5.4 months |
| 15+ | 7.2 months | 4.3 months |

**A small partner who discounts to the floor takes nearly two years to beat
simply having referred the client.** That is a feature. It prices the behaviour
you do not want — a one-client partner buying a discount and putting a cheap
price on the street — without forbidding it. Partners who sell on value rather
than price reach parity in seven months and compound from there.

## MECHANICS

- **Tier measured on active paying CIDs**, monthly.
- **Upgrades apply next billing period.** Cross the threshold, get the rate.
- **Downgrades reviewed quarterly and applied one quarter in arrears.** A single
  churn should not cost a partner their rate mid-book; a sustained decline
  should.
- **The covenant is a contractual term with an audit right**, same instrument as
  the advisory share. Unenforceable in isolation, but it converts an
  unenforceable hope into a term you can point at — which is most of its value.
- **Breach remedy: rate reverts to entry band** for the remainder of the
  contract year. Proportionate, automatic, and does not require terminating a
  partner over a pricing dispute.

## THE TWO CARRIED RECOMMENDATIONS

Taken as accepted under the same silence rule as the Section B sweep. Say
otherwise and they change.

1. **Tier 1 client discount is FIRST YEAR ONLY**, matching the commission
   window. Perpetual would carry $5,994/year forever on every referred client
   for a one-off introduction. **The brochure line needs "first year" added** —
   it currently says only "a ten per cent discount for the client you bring."
2. **Advisory revenue scope is engagement-bounded** — advisory billed against a
   pilot, quarterly cycle or programme running on an AXIOM CID. The only scope
   whose denominator is observable without an audit right.
