# AXIOM FAIR USE POLICY
## Draft for founder review · 31 July 2026

Written to be published as-is. Plain English, no metering, no thresholds a
customer has to track.

**The principle it encodes:** AXIOM limits **scope**, not **volume**. Nothing a
customer does within the intended scope is ever counted, capped or charged.

---

# Fair Use

AXIOM is priced per company, not per person. Your plan includes unlimited users
because we would rather your whole transformation team used the platform than
have you decide who can afford a seat.

This policy explains the small number of boundaries that make that possible. It
is short on purpose. If you are using AXIOM to run your own company's
transformation, you will never encounter any of it.

## One company per workspace

Each AXIOM workspace models one company — its financial statements, its
organisation, its assessment cycle, its transformation.

If you run a group, a holding company, or a portfolio, each company needs its own
workspace. This is not a licensing technicality: a workspace holding two
companies produces one blended model, which gives you worse analysis than two
clear ones.

If you are not sure where the line falls in your structure, ask us. We would
rather help you set it up properly than have you work around it.

## Your users are your own people

Unlimited users means everyone in your organisation: executives, managers,
project owners, and the employees you invite to take part in an assessment. It
also covers your board, and advisers and contractors acting for you.

It does not extend to giving access to another organisation, or to providing
AXIOM as a service to your own clients. If you want to do that, we have a partner
programme built for it — talk to us.

Accounts are personal. Please do not share credentials.

## Assessments are for your own people

You can invite as many of your own people to an assessment cycle as you like.

AXIOM does not currently support surveying people outside your organisation —
your customers, or your suppliers. That is a deliberate limit rather than a
capacity one, and it is about protecting the people being surveyed and the
deliverability of everyone's invitations.

**You can still bring that data in.** If you run customer or partner research
elsewhere, you can import the results and use them alongside everything else in
AXIOM. See *Bringing in your own data*.

## Your data is yours to export

Export whatever you need for your own business: board packs, analysis, reporting,
records.

What we ask is that you do not redistribute AXIOM's output as a product or
service of your own, or extract it in bulk to rebuild the platform elsewhere.

## Automated access

Please do not use scripts or automated tools to extract data at scale, or to
query AI features programmatically.

If you want automated access, we would like to hear about it — there is usually a
better answer than a script, and we will not make it difficult.

## If something looks unusual

If your usage looks like it falls outside this policy, **we will contact you and
ask.**

We will not suspend your account, throttle your platform, or add a charge to your
invoice without speaking to you first. In almost every case the explanation is a
misconfiguration, a bulk import that went further than intended, or a growing
business that needs a different arrangement — and all three are conversations,
not enforcement.

The only exceptions are security incidents, unlawful use, and non-payment, where
we may need to act before we can reach you.

## Changes

We will give you 30 days' notice of any change to this policy, and any change
will apply from your next renewal — never mid-term.

# NOTES FOR SAMIR — NOT FOR PUBLICATION

**What this deliberately does not contain:** any number. No user ceiling, no
query limit, no storage cap, no export threshold. The moment one appears, a CFO
has to track it and a CXO starts rationing — which is the friction you removed.

**The enforcement clause is the important one.** "We will contact you and ask,
we will not suspend or charge without speaking to you" is a real commitment and
it should hold. It is also the clause that makes the absence of numbers safe: you
retain judgement, you have simply promised to exercise it in conversation rather
than automatically.

**Three clauses carry commercial weight beyond fair use:**

1. *One company per workspace* protects CID expansion, which is the growth
   vector. It is stated as being for the customer's benefit, which it also
   genuinely is.
2. *Not providing AXIOM as a service to your own clients* routes consultancies to
   the partner programme rather than letting them quietly resell a single seat.
3. *Not redistributing output as a product* protects against a customer packaging
   AXIOM analysis as their own offering.

**The external-survey clause is written as protection, not restriction** — "about
protecting the people being surveyed and the deliverability of everyone's
invitations." True, and it reads better than a capability gap.

**30 days' notice, effective at renewal** matters given annual terms. A policy
that can change mid-term is not a term.
