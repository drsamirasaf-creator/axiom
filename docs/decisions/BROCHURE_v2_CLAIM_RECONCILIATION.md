## RULING NEEDED

Three ways to close this, in descending order of my preference:

**1. Hold the brochure until V1.0 ships.** Cleanest. The document is correct on
the day it becomes true, and no clause needs editing. Costs nothing if there is
no external commercial clock — and the platform is deliberately dark pending the
v1.0 relaunch gate, so there may not be one.

**2. Ship now with the binding sentence changed.** Replace *"Every capability
listed is live in the platform"* with wording that distinguishes shipped from
committed, and mark the roadmap rows visibly. Honest, but a brochure with
asterisks sells worse than one without, and the marks would need removing later
anyway.

**3. Ship a cut-down brochure now.** Sections A and B removed, ~4 pages. Fully
true today. Rebuild to nine pages at V1.0.

**I would not ship the current file externally under any circumstance**, because
the page-8 automated-check claim and the page-3 "live in the platform" line are
specific and checkable, and a CFO audience is exactly the audience that checks.
Internally, as a build target and a design reference, it is fine as it stands —
arguably its best use, since it defines what V1.0 means.

## THE USEFUL SIDE OF THIS

The brochure is now the **most complete statement of V1.0 scope in existence** —
more complete than the ledger, because it was written from the outcome backwards
rather than the feature list forwards.

Section A is therefore a build queue, ordered by how much brochure it unblocks:

1. **§7r ratio library** — unblocks p3 entirely, plus p2 and p7 rows. Registry
   already written; largest surface per unit of work.
2. **§7m Part 2 initiative execution** — unblocks 8 rows and the whole
   accountability argument on p6. Nothing else in the document depends on it,
   so it is fully parallelisable.
3. **CXO priorities registry** — 2 rows, but it is a *priced* Prescience
   differentiator. Currently sold and absent.
4. **Survey designer** — 1 row, and §4i is already designed.
5. **Distress screens** — 2 rows, formulas already in the registry.
6. **`/free-pilot` page + partner mechanics** — commercial surface, no
   dependency on any of the above.

Note that (3) is small work carrying disproportionate commercial weight: it
appears in the Prescience column on the pricing page, which means it is
load-bearing for a $7,000/month upgrade.
