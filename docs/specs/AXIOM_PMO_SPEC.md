**CLAUDE MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT**

**AXIOM ENTERPRISE PROJECT MANAGEMENT OFFICE MODULE**

**Execute State --- Enterprise Strategy Execution and Project
Intelligence Platform**

You are acting as a world-class enterprise software architect, product
strategist, UX designer, quantitative analyst, project portfolio
management specialist, AI engineer, database architect, and senior
full-stack developer.

Design and implement a production-grade **Project Management Office
module for AXIOM**, positioned within AXIOM's **EXECUTE** state of the
business.

This must not be a conventional task-management application. It must
become AXIOM's most powerful execution-management and CXO
decision-support feature.

The module must connect:

-   Strategic priorities

-   Stakeholder recommendations

-   Departmental objectives

-   Approved initiatives

-   Projects and programs

-   Budgets and resources

-   Milestones and deliverables

-   Risks and dependencies

-   Financial and operational outcomes

-   Strategic value creation

-   Organizational performance

-   Executive accountability

The purpose is to give CXOs a real-time, enterprise-wide view of whether
the company's strategy is actually being executed through the right
projects, by the right people, at the right cost, with the right
expected outcomes.

**2. CORE BUSINESS OBJECTIVE**

The PMO module must answer the following CXO questions instantly:

-   What major projects are currently active?

-   Which projects are strategically important?

-   Which projects are delivering the expected benefits?

-   Which projects are delayed, over budget, under-resourced, or at
    risk?

-   Which projects are creating the greatest enterprise value?

-   Which projects should be accelerated?

-   Which projects should be paused, redesigned, merged, or terminated?

-   Which departments are executing effectively?

-   Which project managers are consistently delivering results?

-   Which projects depend on one another?

-   Which bottlenecks threaten multiple strategic priorities?

-   Which stakeholder-recommended projects have received the strongest
    assessor support?

-   Which recommendations have not yet been reviewed?

-   Which approved projects have not yet been staffed or launched?

-   Are projects collectively advancing the company toward its desired
    future state?

-   Are the expected benefits appearing in actual operational and
    financial performance?

-   Is the project portfolio within the company's financial,
    human-capital, risk, and execution capacity?

The module must transform project management from administrative
reporting into a strategic, quantitative, and predictive management
capability.

**4. ORGANIZATIONAL PMO MODEL**

The system must support several PMO operating models simultaneously.

**4.1 Enterprise PMO**

The company may appoint an overall:

-   Chief Project Officer

-   Enterprise PMO Director

-   Enterprise PMO Manager

-   Transformation Office Director

-   Strategy Execution Director

The Enterprise PMO can oversee all programs and projects across the
company.

Enterprise PMO responsibilities may include:

-   Creating enterprise projects

-   Reviewing all departmental projects

-   Maintaining governance standards

-   Monitoring portfolio health

-   Reviewing project status

-   Escalating issues

-   Reassigning project ownership where authorized

-   Reviewing cross-functional dependencies

-   Managing enterprise programs

-   Preparing CXO and board reporting

-   Maintaining project templates

-   Reviewing project closure

-   Validating benefits realization

-   Maintaining the corporate project portfolio

**4.2 Departmental PMO**

Each department may appoint one or more:

-   Departmental PMO Managers

-   Departmental Project Coordinators

-   Program Managers

-   Project Portfolio Managers

A Departmental PMO Manager may:

-   Create and maintain projects for the department

-   Update project status

-   Assign departmental project managers, subject to approval rules

-   Maintain milestones, budgets, risks, and dependencies

-   Submit projects for departmental CXO approval

-   Coordinate project reporting

-   Escalate issues to the departmental CXO

-   Collaborate with the Enterprise PMO

**4.3 Hybrid PMO**

The system must support a hybrid model where:

-   Departmental PMO Managers maintain project data.

-   Enterprise PMO establishes standards and monitors the full
    portfolio.

-   Departmental CXOs approve departmental projects.

-   Enterprise CXOs approve enterprise-wide or high-value projects.

-   Cross-functional programs are governed jointly.

**4.4 No Dedicated Departmental PMO**

Where a department does not have a PMO Manager:

-   The project manager may maintain the project data.

-   The department head may maintain project data.

-   The Enterprise PMO may assume responsibility.

-   A designated project coordinator may be assigned.

Every project must have a clearly identified **data-maintenance owner**.

**53. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA**

The module is acceptable only when:

1.  A company can configure an Enterprise PMO.

2.  Each department can appoint one or more Departmental PMO Managers.

3.  Each project has a clear data-maintenance owner.

4.  Stakeholder recommendations can become recommended projects.

5.  Recommended projects show assessor star ratings.

6.  A departmental CXO can review a recommended project.

7.  A departmental CXO can accept, reject, defer, merge, or escalate it.

8.  Acceptance creates a draft project record.

9.  The CXO can assign or reassign the project manager.

10. The project manager or PMO can maintain project data.

11. Each project records objective, scope, budget, milestones,
    stakeholders, timeline, dependencies, and critical success factors.

12. Each project links to strategy, objectives, Key Results, and KPIs.

13. Executives can view the complete enterprise portfolio.

14. Departmental CXOs can view departmental execution.

15. Project health is calculated independently by AXIOM.

16. Risks, issues, dependencies, decisions, and changes are traceable.

17. Benefits are tracked beyond project completion.

18. Budget and schedule variances are calculated correctly.

19. All approvals and changes are auditable.

20. Tenant and departmental access controls are enforced.

21. Dashboards clearly identify where executive action is required.

22. The interface remains fast and intuitive at enterprise scale.

23. All significant workflows have automated tests.

24. No critical console, API, database, permission, or validation errors
    remain.

**55. FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLES**

The completed PMO module must embody these principles:

1.  **Strategy must be traceable to execution.**

2.  **Every project must have clear ownership.**

3.  **Every project must have measurable outcomes.**

4.  **Every material project must have a valid business case.**

5.  **Every project must show its budget, schedule, risks, and
    dependencies.**

6.  **Stakeholder recommendations must not disappear into static
    reports.**

7.  **CXOs must be able to convert strong recommendations into governed
    projects.**

8.  **Project status must be independently challenged by AXIOM
    analytics.**

9.  **Benefits must be measured after delivery.**

10. **Executives must see where intervention is required.**

11. **The company must understand whether it has the capacity to execute
    its portfolio.**

12. **AXIOM must become the system connecting strategic intent to
    measurable corporate outcomes.**

The final module should make it possible for a CEO, CFO, COO, or
departmental CXO to open AXIOM and understand within minutes:

-   What the company is trying to execute

-   Why each major project exists

-   What it will cost

-   Who is accountable

-   Whether it is on track

-   What risks threaten it

-   What decisions are required

-   What benefits are expected

-   Whether those benefits are actually being achieved

Build this as a major enterprise product, not as a basic project
tracker.
